Questions

Why do some products say Rx?

They're prescription-strength. You order them like anything else; checkout adds four quick health questions, and a licensed clinician reviews the order before it ships.

Are these products for men or women?

Both. Skin and hair respond to treatment the same way regardless of who's using them. We describe products by what they do, not who they're for.

Why these six products?

Each one is the best at what it does. We only stock one product for each job.

Can I combine products?

Usually, yes. Each product page lists what it pairs well with and what to keep on separate nights. Our guides cover full routines.

How do I change or cancel a subscription?

From your account.

Is this medical advice?

No. Our guides are general information. For persistent, sudden or worsening skin and hair concerns, see a doctor or dermatologist.
