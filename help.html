Returns

Return anything within 30 days, even if it's opened. Use the prepaid label in your box, or request one by replying to your order email. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 working days of the parcel reaching us.

If a product didn't agree with your skin, tell us — we'll refund it and suggest a gentler alternative if there is one.

Our stock promise

Every product page shows how many of the last 365 days it was in stock. We hold at least eight weeks of cover on every product, and more on subscription favourites.

If something does run short, its page stays live with a firm restock date. You can reserve it at no charge — we only take payment when it ships. Subscribers get first allocation.

Samples

Choose two complimentary samples in your bag before checkout. They're sized for a one-week trial, so you can test a product before committing to a full size or subscription.

Questions

Are these products for men or women?

Both. Skin and hair respond to actives the same way regardless of who's using them. We describe products by what they do, not who they're for.

Why do you list exact percentages?

Because strength determines results and tolerance. Our customers compare formulas, and they should be able to. Full INCI lists are on every product page.

Can I combine products?

Usually, yes. Each product page lists what it pairs well with and what to keep on separate nights. Our guides cover full routines.

How do I pause or cancel a subscription?

From the link in any subscription email, or from your account. One click, no phone calls, no retention offers.

Is this medical advice?

No. Our guides are general information. For persistent, sudden or worsening skin and hair concerns, see a doctor or dermatologist.
